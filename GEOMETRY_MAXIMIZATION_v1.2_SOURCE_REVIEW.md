- S20–S22 are recommendations/audit records with no governing adoption effect. Their current packet-level unresolved status does not erase the separate proved FH-REF-1 theorem.
- S13 is a governance notice. The broader claim that no SW-1 statistic exists is corrected by S01/S31 §60, which does define it.

The Word source has 16 tables, 368 text-bearing paragraphs including table paragraphs, zero native Office Math objects, and six images. Its text and embedded images were inspected. No page-layout rendering pass is claimed. The source's behavioral charts and narrative are not substitutes for their absent underlying records.
