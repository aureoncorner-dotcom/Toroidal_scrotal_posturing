## Physical balance

Declare the system boundary and time interval. With every energy transfer counted once:

$$
\Delta E_{\mathrm{store}}=E_{\mathrm{in}}-E_{\mathrm{out}}.
$$

All terms are energy in joules. Outgoing energy already includes useful physical work. Include heat, work, and matter-carried energy as applicable. [MIT, conservation laws](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/FALL/thermodynamics/notes/node19.html).

Entropy generation $S_{\mathrm{gen}}\geq0$ has units J/K; it cannot be added directly to work in J. It is not a measure of verbosity or social friction. [OpenStax, entropy](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/15-6-entropy-and-the-second-law-of-thermodynamics-disorder-and-the-unavailability-of-energy).

Relative to a fixed environment temperature $T_0$, destroyed work potential is $B_{\mathrm{dest}}=T_0S_{\mathrm{gen}}$. Recover energy or material streams; entropy generation is not a fuel source. Internal recovery is not new energy input. [MIT, unavailable energy](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/FALL/thermodynamics/notes/node49.html).

## Coordination and capacity

For each resource $r$, keep a separate account in a single unit:

$$
m^{(r)}=a^{(r)}-u_{\mathrm{task}}^{(r)}-u_{\mathrm{coord}}^{(r)}
-u_{\mathrm{maint}}^{(r)}-u_{\mathrm{regen}}^{(r)}.
$$

Available resource minus attributed uses gives its margin. Uses cover task delivery, coordination, maintenance, and regeneration; prevent overlap and state whether the account is planned, estimated, or observed. Do not add money, joules, and hours together. Positive margin means resource headroom, not universal health. Useful output has its own quality description.

For each named capacity:

$$
k_a(t+\Delta t)=k_a(t)+g_a-d_a.
$$

Gains and losses use the capacity's own units. This is bookkeeping, not a predictive law. Spending on regeneration does not prove that capacity improved. Qualitative capacities can remain descriptions.

| Region | Meaning within a named boundary and interval |
|---|---|
| Extraction | A named stock or capacity declines. |
| Maintenance | Specified service and capacity remain within declared bounds. |
| Surplus | A resource has headroom after declared allocations. |
| Regeneration | A named capacity improves on net. |

Regions can coexist. “Resonance” and “Garden” are optional metaphors. Zero energy accumulation is not bare survival, and surplus does not prove dynamical stability. Preserve mixed outcomes and unknowns.
