## What the newer mathematics adds


- **A constructive measurement repair.** Equal endpoint measurements can conceal different interior shapes and contact regions. One specified interior measurement recovers the parameter in the declared profile family, with an explicit error bound.
- **Recovery and conditioning are separate.** A unique quartic contact minimum can be extremely sensitive near zero tilt. Strong convexity supplies a quantitative bound, including boundary minima.
- **Error and coverage bounds.** A uniform surface-height error ε gives a 2ε bound after each minimum is subtracted, and nested bounds on the near-gap regions. A sampled check extends to a global bound only with a valid Lipschitz constant and domain coverage.
- **Complete geometric checks.** Trace data do not determine transverse curvature. Local envelope tangency requires end caps, trimming and a global non-interference argument. Physical curvature requires units and the coordinate metric.
- **Additional gear calculations.** Added join regularity, involute center-distance/contact-ratio calculations, the full backlash reversal angle, relative material cycle counts and explicit limits on interpreting a gap contour as loaded contact.


The older comparison's endpoint wording is corrected: the stated half-arch has a regular one-sided C¹ endpoint with unbounded curvature; the two-sided continuation has a cusp.
