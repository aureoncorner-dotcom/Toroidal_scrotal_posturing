1 Calculus of loci
These are set identities. They do not require topology.
1.1 Product of witnesses
Let (W=(W_1,W_2):D\to Y_1\times Y_2).
Lemma D1. (N_{(W_1,W_2)}(\pi)=N_{W_1}(\pi)\cup N_{W_2}(\pi)).
Proof. A fiber is heterogeneous for the pair iff it is heterogeneous for at least one factor. (\square)
Corollary. Joint recovery fails exactly on the union of the separate defects. Cleaning one witness does not clean the other.
1.2 Coarsening the witness
Let (f:Y\to Z) and set (W'=f\circ W).
Lemma D2. (N_{W'}(\pi)\subseteq N_W(\pi)).
Proof. Distinct (W') values require distinct (W) values. (\square)
A coarser question can descend after a finer question has already failed. This is the exact reading of New geometry §5: (E_{\rm opt}) may descend while ((E_g,E_b)) does not. It is also the exact reading of §15.1: the existential Semaev relation can descend while specified signed output does not.
1.3 Refining the observation
If (\pi=r\circ\pi') with (\pi':D\to Q'), then already (New geometry Prop. 14.4)
[
r\bigl(N_W(\pi')\bigr)\subseteq N_W(\pi).
]
Lemma D3 (no new coarse defect). If (q\notin N_W(\pi)), then no point of (r^{-1}(q)) lies in (N_W(\pi')).
A refinement can split a bad fiber. It cannot invent a collision whose coarse image was clean.
1.4 Restricting the domain
Let (C\subseteq D) and write (\pi|_C), (W|_C).
Lemma D4. (N_{W|_C}(\pi|_C)\subseteq N_W(\pi)\cap\pi(C)).
The inclusion can be strict. A collision that used a point of (D\setminus C) disappears. This is not descent on (D). It is descent on a smaller source. Record the restriction separately (Working Master §2; New geometry §15.3).
1.5 Predictive locus is a static locus
Let (U:D\to D) be a declared deterministic update.
Lemma D5. An autonomous retained update on (Q) exists if and only if (N_{\pi\circ U}(\pi)=\varnothing).
Proof. (\pi(x)=\pi(y)) implies (\pi(Ux)=\pi(Uy)) iff (\pi\circ U) is constant on every (\pi)-fiber iff that non-descent locus is empty. (\square)
Finite-horizon loss (\delta_h^B) is defined using the full future output path (B_1,\ldots,B_h), or its law with total-variation distance. Under that convention increasing (h) cannot shrink the diameter, because marginalization cannot increase total variation. A terminal-only output law at time h need not have monotone diameter. Recovering the next deterministic output (B) tests (N_{B\circ U}(\pi)=\varnothing); (N_B(\pi)=\varnothing) concerns the present. Neither next-output recovery nor present-output recovery alone implies (N_{\pi\circ U}(\pi)=\varnothing).
The Sophie Germain example is D5, not a new closure theorem: (r(3)=r(5)) while (r(U(3))\ne r(U(5))), so (3,5) witness (N_{r\circ U}(r)\ne\varnothing).


2 Five defect kinds
Each kind has a test, a repair that is legal for that kind, and a repair that is illegal. The numerals below retain the meanings of Typed Defects v0.2; record the source version, explicit test, and descriptive label. They are diagnostic patterns, not a disjoint or exhaustive classification.
Kind I — Operation loss on a surviving relation
Test. Two maps (W_{\rm out}) and (W_{\rm rel}) on the same (D), with (N_{W_{\rm rel}}(\pi)=\varnothing) and (N_{W_{\rm out}}(\pi)\ne\varnothing).
Legal repair. Retain the extra coordinate that splits the (W_{\rm out}) fiber (e.g. the sign/(y)-coordinate).
Illegal repair. Treat vanishing of (W_{\rm rel}) as recovery of (W_{\rm out}).
Instance. New geometry §15.1. On affine pairs of (E(\mathbb F_{13}):y^2=x^3+2x+2) with affine sum, (\pi(P,Q)=(x(P),x(Q))) loses (x(P+Q)) at ((P,Q)) versus ((P,-Q)), while the Semaev relation (S_3(x_1,x_2,x_3)=0) still records existence of some signs. Existential relation and specified output are different witnesses. Lemma D2 says the coarser one can descend.
Kind II — Unit / character loss
Test. (\pi) factors through an ideal class, a norm, or another map that kills a unit group, and (W) is a logarithm or character that sees that unit.
Legal repair. Add a character of declared rank, kernel, modulus, and precision (Schirokauer-type local log on a stated two-element domain, or the full map under its own hypotheses).
Illegal repair. Promote a coefficient that separates one pair into a global recovery theorem. Illegal also: treat (\gcd(h,\ell)=1) as optional when writing (h^{-1}\bmod\ell).
Instance. New geometry §15.2. In (\mathbb Z[\sqrt{2}]), ((1)=(1+\sqrt{2})) as ideals; reduction (\sqrt{2}\mapsto 3) into (\mathbb F_7) gives distinct base-3 logs modulo 3. The local character (L) separates that pair. Pair separation is not Leopoldt, not unit-rank sufficiency on a larger domain, and not NFS-DL.
Kind III — Subgroup-relative injectivity
Test. A homomorphism (\varphi:G\to H) and a declared subgroup (C\le G) satisfy (\ker\varphi\cap C={e}) while (\ker\varphi\ne{e}).
Legal repair. Restrict the source to (C) and record the restriction (Lemma D4). On (C), the retained homomorphism is injective, so every total witness descends there and no multi-point fiber defect remains. This does not imply injectivity on the ambient group.
Illegal repair. Infer injectivity on (G), infer a computational speedup, or identify this with Weil restriction / GHS / Jacobian HCDLP. Those are separate assertions.
Instance. New geometry §15.3.
Kind IV — Discrete sector defect on disconnected fibers
Test. Under the declared smooth hypotheses on the domain, maps and fibers, (A_W=dW|_{\ker d\pi}=0) implies constancy on each connected fiber component, yet the componentwise constants disagree. An arbitrary discrete membrane collision does not establish these differential hypotheses.
Legal repair. Retain a discrete sheet label, or pass to (\mathsf S_W).
Illegal repair. Treat a vanishing vertical differential as descent when fibers are not connected (New geometry Thm 14.7 converse fails).
Kind V — Representability gap outside the image
Test. Declare a representation (j:A\to Z) and a needed object (z\notin j(A)). This is an outside-image failure for that map. It is distinct from (N_W(\pi)), which concerns unequal witness values inside an attained fiber; injectivity of an associated observation is a possible example, not a defining requirement.
Legal repair. Name the missing object and change the representable class or representation map so that it is actually attained. Merely adding unused codomain points changes no image. Hidden Quotient example C illustrates the outside-image issue; point evaluation in example A is a different repair, where (N_W) was the whole of (Q).
Illegal repair. Call the gap (N_W(\pi)). Non-descent is a collision inside an attained fiber. A missing dual functional is outside the image.
These five patterns are the original Typed Defects v0.2 vocabulary, not a partition of every possible failure. Later Geometry sources also use Kind VI for a spectral witness family: declare the state-to-operator or state-to-path assignment, operator domains, boundary conditions, regularization and functional. A nonzero spectral value alone is insufficient; non-descent requires equal retained labels with different functional values or an equivalent proof. No toroidal operator assignment is declared by this vocabulary.

4 Sector space as the typed attachment
(\mathsf S_W(\pi)) already carries every attained pair ((q,y)). Typed defects read off it as follows.
Applicable Kind I, II and IV collisions: some (r)-fiber of (\mathsf S_W\to Q) has more than one point. Kind III instead identifies an injective restriction to (C); on that restricted source every such fiber has one point.
Which collision pattern applies depends on the declared test and the extra coordinate that separates the witness values, such as sign, unit character or a sheet index. Restriction to a subgroup is a separate domain operation.
Kind V concerns a missing object in a separately declared representation map. It can coexist with clean witness descent, but its outside-image test is distinct from the fibers of (\mathsf S_W\to Q).
Canonical refinement (\pi_W) resolves every attained-fiber collision for this particular (W) by retaining (W) itself. Every record determining both (\pi) and (W) factors onto (\mathsf S_W=\operatorname{im}(\pi,W)), making it coarsest in information order. It does not select a mechanistic coordinate, minimize dimension, bytes or measurement cost, or make a predictor available at runtime. A Kind III restriction and a Kind V outside-image repair remain separate operations.
Context attachment is a map (c:D\to C) such that (N_W((\pi,c))=\varnothing) while (N_W(\pi)\ne\varnothing). An inclusion-minimal such (c), among a declared menu of available coordinates, is the empirical compiler’s (S^*(W,D,C)). Geometry supplies the test. It does not supply the menu.

This is an ordinary same-fiber witness collision; a new numbered defect kind is unnecessary. Adding the attainable interior measurement G_w(1/2,0;0)=5−15w/4 repairs this specified family.

Similarly, a local contact witness and a global no-penetration witness are separate targets; apply D1 to their joint recovery only after defining them on the same domain.


Trace equality is weaker than surface equality: u²+v² and u²+2v² have equal values and gradients along v=0, but transverse second derivatives 2 and 4. Cross-cutting diagnostics beside the versioned mathematical families
Keep the original I–V vocabulary and later spectral VI attached to their declared versions and hypotheses. The following checks are additional diagnostics, not an exhaustive new partition or renumbering of those families.
Observer-state conflation: a changed observation or classification is attributed to the underlying system without accounting for O(h,m), capture coverage and observer/transform state. Repair by retaining those coordinates; a change alone does not establish a source-state transition.
Unsupported provenance inference: events from distinct evidence spaces are treated as one request, session, native fragment, actor or write without a supported binding. Repair with typed source relations and explicit missing joins. Missing linkage alone is not a same-fiber counterexample for an unobserved target.
Disposition error: a criterion-supported finding is demoted because its cause is unknown, or a constant UNRESOLVED label is treated as automatically correct. Apply the claim’s evidence criterion as well as fiber constancy. Criterion-preserving evidence updates must preserve the warranted disposition.
Selection or delivery failure: selected text, completed task content and the actually delivered object are collapsed. Bind selection to the request and source spans; check completion and active constraints; compare delivery to the validated candidate. An offline repair does not erase historical compliance failures.
These diagnostics can coexist with exact descent, a conditioning problem or an outside-image failure. Label each claimed obstruction by its actual witness and test.
