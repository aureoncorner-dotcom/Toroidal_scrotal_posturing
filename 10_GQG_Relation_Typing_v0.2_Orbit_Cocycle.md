# GQG Relation Typing and No-Descent Notice v0.1

## 1. Purpose

GQG's no-silent-transport rule applies when a claim is transported between witness-relative quotient structures as though a well-defined descended map exists. It does not require a descent proof for every design choice, motivation, analogy, or calibration comparison.

The repair is therefore to type every important cross-document relation. A relation explicitly typed as non-derivational must not inherit claims from its source object.

---

## 2. Relation Types

| Relation type | Meaning | Certificate required? | Permitted inheritance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `DERIVED_DESCENT` | A declared map preserves the relevant witness-induced equivalence and induces a well-defined quotient map | Yes | Only the properties proved by the certificate |
| `CONSTRUCTOR_CHOICE` | A microscopic object is deliberately selected to test a conditional mechanism | No descent certificate; design specification required | No uniqueness, inevitability, or source-generation claim |
| `MOTIVATED_BY` | Structure in one object suggests features of another | No | No equality, equivalence, or physical-generation claim |
| `CALIBRATED_AGAINST` | A benchmark checks implementation, observable conventions, limiting behavior, or scale sensitivity | Benchmark interface required | Only the checks explicitly named in the interface |
| `COMPARED_WITH` | Results are displayed together under declared conventions | No descent certificate; comparison specification required | No parameter or verdict transfer |
| `FORBIDDEN_TRANSPORT` | A tempting inference is explicitly disallowed | Not applicable | None |

If a relation is later upgraded to `DERIVED_DESCENT`, the map, source and target witnesses, equivalence-preservation proof, scope, and version must be supplied before the upgraded claim is used.

**Declaration:** The discrete source may motivate structural questions or notation in Theory v0.1. The present project does not claim that the recurrence physically generates the continuum gauge field, uniquely determines the continuum action, or descends to the continuum quotient through a proved map.

## 4. Benchmark Interface Record

Every `CALIBRATED_AGAINST` relation should name:

1. the exact benchmark artifact and version;
2. the ensemble and boundary/global-sector construction;
3. the observable definitions and normalizations;
4. the benchmark parameter values used only in that lane;
5. the expected validation outputs and tolerances;
6. the production quantities explicitly forbidden from inheritance; and
7. the consequence of benchmark failure.

This record is a calibration interface, not a descent certificate.

---

## 5. No-Silent-Upgrade Rule

A relation may not silently change type. In particular:

- `MOTIVATED_BY` may not be written later as “derived from”;
- `CONSTRUCTOR_CHOICE` may not be written later as “the lattice realization” without a uniqueness claim and proof;
- `CALIBRATED_AGAINST` may not become parameter transport; and
- repeated empirical agreement may not be redescribed as quotient equivalence.

Any proposed upgrade requires a new, versioned relation record and regeneration of every downstream claim that depends on it.

---

## 7. Non-Claims

This notice does not:

- place design choices outside epistemic audit;
- treat motivation as evidence;
- weaken the descent requirement when a quotient transport is actually claimed;
- validate the chosen microscopic model; or
- allow comparator data to decide production physics.

Its sole purpose is to prevent both silent transport and the opposite error of demanding a descent proof for a relation that expressly makes no descent claim.

## 8. Orbit/cocycle relation types

Append the following types:

| Relation type | Meaning | Required record | Evidence inheritance |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EXACT_QUOTIENT` | A declared source map has a proved kernel/quotient identity | source, map, kernel, target | only the proved quotient statement |
| `DERIVED_QUOTIENT_COMPONENT` | A source-level update has a computed quotient-valued component | complete operator receipt and quotient computation | sector-change statement only |
| `DRIVEN_COCYCLE` | Quotient increments concatenate and transport a label under retained driving state | increment definition, driver, concatenation law | no residual autonomy |
| `AUGMENTED_STATE_CLOSURE` | Adding retained state yields an explicit closed update on a declared graph/domain | sufficient-state graph and update map | formal model closure only |
| `FORMAL_PROPOSAL_ONLY` | A complete future test is specified but not executed | frozen schema and non-claim boundary | no empirical support |
| `EMPIRICAL_INSTANTIATION` | A formal structure is claimed to occur in data or a platform | independent observation and validation | only the evidenced instantiation |
