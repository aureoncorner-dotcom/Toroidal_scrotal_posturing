2. The geometry and its witnesses
A three-phase model θ∈T³ has two independent relative phases:
φ=(θ2−θ1,θ3−θ1)∈T².
The quotient removes common rotation. Keep absolute phase whenever the selected witness needs it. The collective phase carries a clock hand; the relative state diagnoses lock, slip, or drift.
A directed witness preserves signed geometry; a shell witness forgets orientation. A recurrence means return under the specified witness and tolerance or score, not identity of the full sky. Historical and symbolic views are downstream records.
Lift continuous relative paths after choosing an initial lift. Weave winding m∈ℤ² records that path history. It is distinct from gauge winding W∈ℤ³ and parity q=W mod 2; shared terminology supplies no map between them.
Recurrence, crossing, slip, lock, and tick are different event classes. A tick requires a declared hand and oriented crossing rule. Sampling must resolve the chosen event; wrapped samples alone can hide turns or direction.
3. What the weave actually establishes
The exploratory coupled comparator is
θ̇i=ωi+(K/3)Σj sin(θj−θi).
On the invariant branch θ1=θ2, the relative phase a=θ3−θ1 obeys ȧ=δ−K sin a. It is a model law with declared parameters, not an ephemeris-derived planetary interaction.
The collective overlap squared is C(a)=(5+4 cos a)/9. States a=±π/2 both give C=5/9, but their derivatives differ by −8δ/9. At δ=0.003 and K=0.0015, the rates are −1/1500 and +1/500. Thus the same overlap can be falling or rising under the same law. For sufficiently small positive sampling intervals, overlap is not an autonomous predictive state on the full symmetric circle. 8. Geometric paths, relative phase and the physical clock


The gear comparator makes the clock distinction explicit. For a configuration path b(q) traversed with q=q(t), db/dt=b′(q)q̇ and d²b/dt²=b″(q)q̇²+b′(q)q̈. A monotone reparametrization of the same interval preserves its path image, but generally changes duration, speed, acceleration and sampled event frequency. Equality of a path image therefore does not recover a temporal witness.


For a pattern with N identical lobes rotating at signed rate n_w relative to material rotating at n_f, the ideal encounter rate is N|n_w−n_f| per minute when n is in rpm. The difference is a relative-phase derivative. It is not a calibration of planetary or physical clocks. Return maps additionally need their sampling and crossing conventions, including the treatment of stops and signed windings.
