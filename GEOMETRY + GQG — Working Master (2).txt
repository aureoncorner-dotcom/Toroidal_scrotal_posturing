1. Retain the distinction the claim needs
Declare a source domain D, retained map π, and witness W. Work on attained labels Q=π(D). The non-descent locus is
N_W(π)={q∈Q : some x,y∈π⁻¹(q) satisfy W(x)≠W(y)}.
A same-label pair with different witnesses shows that W cannot be recovered from that label. The canonical exact witness-resolving refinement is the image of (π,W), with its projection back to Q. It is coarsest for retaining this witness; it need not be a causal state or a practical predictor.
The no-shape result is essential: without additional assumptions, any subset of Q can occur as the non-descent locus. A local collision does not determine its global geometry. Metric witness diameters support tolerance-dependent questions; a tested pair gives a lower bound. Openness, regularity, vertical-derivative criteria, and transport require their stated topological, smooth, connected-fiber, and lifting assumptions. Disconnected fibers may conceal discrete differences. Nonzero witness holonomy implies non-descent under the declared transport; non-descent alone does not establish holonomy.
The ideal string example makes the distinction concrete: at fixed length, (T,μ) and (cT,cμ) have equal pitch but different tension. Keeping μ repairs tension recovery in that model. It supplies no general mechanism claim.
Arithmetic witness examples
On y²=x³+2x+2 over F₁₃, the pairs (P,Q) and (P,−Q), with P=(2,1) and Q=(3,3), have retained coordinates (2,3) but x(P+Q)=12 and x(P−Q)=11. Retaining both y-coordinates recovers the signed inputs and their sum on the declared affine domain; no minimality is claimed. An existential signed-sum relation is a different target from the x-coordinate of that specified sum.
In O=Z[√2], 1 and u=1+√2 generate the same ideal. Under √2↦3 into F₇, their base-3 logarithms modulo 3 are 0 and 1. On D={1,u}, the √2 coefficient c of L(α)=(α⁸−1)/3 mod 3O recovers exactly this target: W=c. Recovery on this two-element domain does not establish global recovery on a larger domain.
Both named constants, Belphegor’s Prime and 42×10⁴²+43, are established primes. The primality qualification concerns what a probable-prime test proves, not the mathematical status of these two integers.

The gear family G_w=20[(1−w)x²+w x⁴]+10y²+tx on [−1,1]² supplies a concrete instance of the common test. At t=0 all w∈[0,1] have endpoint record (20,20), yet their δ=2 near-gap areas range from 0.4442882938 at w=0 to 0.8792167529 at w=1. Those endpoints do not determine this area witness. This exact repair does not settle conditioning. At w=1 the unique interior minimum is x*(t)=−cuberoot(t/80), with unbounded derivative at zero tilt. 2. Predictive closure and finite-horizon loss
For deterministic U:D→D, an autonomous retained update exists exactly when
π(x)=π(y) ⇒ π(Ux)=π(Uy).
For a stochastic kernel P, require equal next-observation laws for every measurable target set whenever the retained labels agree. A transition table fitted to one trace does not establish this universal condition.
For a fixed output B, retained record r, kernel, clock, and horizon h, define δh(z) as the supremum of total-variation distances between full joint output-path laws Law(B(X₁),…,B(Xh)) from states in r⁻¹(z). Increasing h cannot decrease this path-law diameter, because marginalization cannot increase total variation; terminal-only laws Law(B(Xh)) need not have this monotonicity. Refining r cannot increase the diameter within a refined fiber. When B=r, zero one-step diameter on every fiber is universal predictive closure. For a different, coarser B, matching its next output alone does not establish autonomy of r.
In deterministic systems, equality of the entire future output sequence defines the coarsest autonomous refinement retaining the output. This characterizes required information; it does not permit future outcomes as predictor inputs.
Worked counterexample: one-step primality is not closure
Let D={n∈Z:n≥2} and U(n)=2n+1. Let q(n) indicate primality and let r(n)=(q(n), q(n)∧q(U(n))); the second component indicates Sophie Germain primality.
Both 3 and 5 have r=(true,true). Their successors are 7 and 11, with r(7)=(true,false) because 15 is composite, and r(11)=(true,true) because 23 is prime. Thus equal retained states can have unequal next retained states: r is not autonomous. This corrects the research packet's claim that the Sophie Germain label is the required exact predictive refinement.
For a chosen observation q, agreement of q(Uᵏ(n)) for every k≥0 defines a forward-invariant equivalence. It characterizes a predictive quotient without guaranteeing a finite or efficiently computable representation. The exact counterexample is an illustration of this section's criterion, not evidence for a new general closure theorem.
3. Exact 39-screen reference model
Phases are cycles. Set α=(3−√5)/2 and τ=15−39α≈0.103325561245899. Then
θn={θ0+nα}, bn=⌊39θn⌋, ρn={39θn}, sn=bn mod 3.
For the departing transition, σn=1_[0,τ)(ρn):
ρn+1=ρn−τ+σn; sn+1=sn−σn mod 3.
The pair phase zn={13θn}=(sn+ρn)/3 rotates by −τ/3. Full bins require θ, strand-plus-slip requires z, and slips require ρ, under the full-circle domain and boundary convention.
The exact count identity is SN=Nτ+ρN−ρ0, hence |SN−Nτ|<1. At θ0=0, the counts are 5/39, 53/507, and 1,034/10,000 departures. Complete slip gaps are 9 or 10. The stationary slip process is nonmixing and has no finite Markov order; the detailed proofs and algebra remain in the source editions.

4. GQG: one operational record, distinct meanings
GQG v0.12 retains separate namespaces for theorem geometry, dyadic admissibility, retained context, and empirical instrumentation. Witness fidelity is claim-relative; fidelity, resolution, and provenance strength are separate properties. A system report records what was said unless its implementation claim is independently supported.
An external residual can establish observable condition dependence under a frozen comparison. It does not reconstruct hidden states or identify the generating mechanism. Source, derived view, detector, decision, action, and outcome remain separately located.
For empirical constraint-response work, determine eligibility before forming the denominator. Retain Object, Standing, Response, and Route separately. Standing, event compliance, window binding rate, and derived demotion are different quantities. UNKNOWN, PARTIAL, exclusions, and censoring remain explicit. Historical counts cannot become successor rates by relabeling. Confirmatory promotion retains the independent, framework-naive sealed first pass and coordinate-level agreement requirements.
The Open-Seam Atlas records typed partial transitions, provenance, discarded distinctions, correction, alternate routes, and path order. Endpoint equality does not establish path equality or a global manifold.
5. Residual dynamics and the clock correction
The unified record R=(δC,δD,δK) carries chart, dyadic, and retained-context comparisons. Each channel declares its own comparator; subtraction and a common metric are not automatic.
Let qR observe a state pair and Ψn advance it. A fixed-time residual update exists only if qR(z)=qR(z′) implies qR(Ψn(z))=qR(Ψn(z′)). A single time-homogeneous map additionally requires agreement across times. Adding a clock can repair cross-time ambiguity; it cannot repair a collision already present at the same time.
UP/DOWN/FLAT/MIXED are coordinate-direction records. Progressive/regressive interpretations require separate polarity and provenance. Residual labels cannot substitute for the empirical eligibility, response, window, and confirmation rules.
6. Hidden Quotient and current limits
The multiplication-representation theorem is relative to the fixed L² witness: its kernel consists of locally null functions, and ∥Mf∥ uses the locally essential supremum. Semifinite reduction and localizable completion are different operations. The v1.7 Radon–Nikodym face retains ν≪λ and strong λ-semifiniteness, with λ=μsf. For finite ν, λ-semifiniteness still remains; absolute continuity alone does not replace that finite-piece condition. Use the full qualified statement in v1.7 when citing the theorem.
Three worked examples distinguish loss of information from missing representations. A: an infinite-mass point invisible to the multiplication witness becomes visible by adding actual point evaluation, giving L∞([0,1])⊕ℂ without changing μ. Keep W(f)=f(p) fixed while enlarging π to (π,W). The alternative singleton encoding loses every point identity, since interval singletons are also null.
B: the full uncountable direct-sum representation is faithful on almost-everywhere classes and localizable despite being non-σ-finite. Restricting observation to countably many slices loses the omitted field. This is a spatial restriction, not a claim about finite temporal memory.
C: counting measure on the countable/co-countable σ-algebra gives an injective but non-surjective inclusion of measurable bounded functions into ℓ∞(I)=(ℓ¹(I))*. A nonmeasurable χJ defines an omitted functional; finite subset projections converge strongly to its multiplier outside the original algebra. This is a completeness/representability gap. It is not N_W(π), which is defined inside the attained image of a total map.
Prediction additionally requires declared dynamics. In the corrected investigation’s identity-update example, the partial record is autonomous even though it cannot recover the full field. Swapping an omitted slice into a retained slice breaks that autonomy; retaining both swapped slices repairs it. These are specified illustrative models, separate from the toroidal kernel.
