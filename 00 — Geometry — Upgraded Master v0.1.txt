1. Reusable mathematical core
Use an attained observation map π:X→Q and a declared target W:X→Y. Define the conflicting-pair relation C_W(π)={(x,x′):π(x)=π(x′), W(x)≠W(x′)} and the ambiguous-label locus N_W(π)={q∈Q:W is nonconstant on π⁻¹(q)}. C is a relation; N is a set of labels.
Descent. W=w∘π for a unique w on Q exactly when C is empty, equivalently Eq(π)⊆Eq(W). This is a set-level result. Measurability, continuity, smoothness, and algebraic structure require additional hypotheses.
Information repair. The attained map x↦(π(x),W(x)) is the coarsest refinement retaining π and resolving W in the factorization order. It specifies required information; it does not acquire W, minimize measurement cost, or certify a practical instrument.
No predetermined shape. For any A⊆Q, take X=(Q×{0})∪(A×{1}), π(q,i)=q, W(q,i)=i. Then N_W(π)=A. The framework alone forces no torus, boundary, cycle, connectedness, or hidden actor.
Failure and coverage. One validated conflicting pair refutes exact descent on its stated domain. No collision found in incomplete coverage does not prove descent. A deterministic relabeling of π cannot recover a distinction π already collapsed.
Retain the calculus for combining witnesses, coarsening targets, refining observations, and restricting domains. Keep static identifiability, deterministic autonomy, stochastic prediction, robustness, and acquisition cost separate. Strong lumpability and observed Markov order under a specified initialization are different claims; a finite software regression suite does not prove an unbounded-domain theorem.

2. Upgrade: claim closure belongs in the core
The strongest new bridge is Claim Closure Geometry 01 — Claim Closure Geometry — Source Edition. For each bounded claim i, retain its proposition, domain, criterion version, relevant evidence rᵢ, stated finding Fᵢ, operational disposition Dᵢ, and action actually taken Tᵢ.
The requirement is Dᵢ=gᵢ(rᵢ). Consequently Eq(rᵢ)⊆Eq(Dᵢ): unchanged relevant evidence and criterion must not produce an incompatible disposition. Constancy alone is insufficient—an always-UNRESOLVED rule can be consistently wrong. The criterion must be independently declared and calibrated to the claim.
If an update U preserves rᵢ and the criterion, then Dᵢ(Ux)=Dᵢ(x). This is a direct consequence of the factorization, not a newly observed property of a platform.
A useful finite counterexample separates an established defect b from knowledge of its cause k. If the declared defect criterion requires disposition b, the gate b∧k fails at b=1,k=0. An unknown cause does not erase that established defect. Conversely, an observable defect does not establish its cause. Causal identification and repair completion need their own claims and criteria.
The resulting repair is operational: preserve the warranted finding, keep causal uncertainty in its own field, and list pending work without using it as an unrelated prerequisite for accepting the finding. Relevant new evidence or a justified criterion revision can change the disposition; record which changed.

7. Unified architecture and practical record
The upgraded architecture has six connected responsibilities:
1. Claim contract: proposition, domain, target witness, evidence criterion, version, eligibility, and exclusions.
2. Observation: retained coordinates, observer/transform state, clock semantics, coverage, and missingness.
3. Provenance: source lineage and supported cross-space bindings, with unresolved joins explicit.
4. Test and repair: exact descent, predictive closure, robustness, or a declared empirical comparison; distinguish acquiring information, restricting the domain, and changing the target.
5. Disposition: warranted finding, remaining causal uncertainty, and repair state as separate fields.
6. Service: request-bound selection, completion, applicable constraints, delivery verification, and historical correction scoring.
For each claim, save: claim ID; kind; source version and locator; domain; observation and target; criterion; relevant evidence; tested coverage; result; mechanism status; repair/action status; proposed next test; and supersession rationale.
Use separate axes for claim kind, result, and execution provenance. “Analytically proved,” “observed failure,” “source reports PASS,” “freshly rerun,” “unresolved,” and “not run” answer different questions. A passed formal test does not promote a mechanism hypothesis into an empirical finding.
